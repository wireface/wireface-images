# Wireface Commercial Agreement

Draft for legal review — 10 October 2026. No binding offer or fixed price.
Complete the order schedule and obtain both parties' signatures before use.

**Parties:** Compsmart [full legal name, company number and address] and
[customer legal name, company number and address].

**Order schedule:** specify licensed products and versions, customer group
entities, intended applications, deployment environments, production and
redistribution scope, embedded Creator functionality, territories, term, fees,
taxes, payment dates, renewal arrangements and authorised contacts. An omitted
field is not deemed unlimited.

1. **Grant.** On payment of agreed fees, Compsmart grants a nonexclusive licence
   within the completed schedule to use the compiled runtime, Creator and bundled
   integrations in production and embed and distribute them in the specified
   finished products. The community threshold does not limit the agreed scope.
   Customers need no separate SDK licence merely to use those products.
2. **Developer redistribution.** Standalone engine or SDK resale, white-label
   toolkits, developer services and development sublicences are excluded unless
   the schedule expressly grants and defines those rights.
3. **Deliverables.** Specify delivery dates and acceptance criteria. Source
   access, source modification, escrow, support, maintenance, service levels and
   future updates are included only where expressly negotiated in a schedule.
   There is no automatic source grant or unlimited future-update promise. The
   compiled packages have the same local features as the community packages.
4. **Ownership and notices.** Each party retains its intellectual property.
   Retain Wireface and third-party notices in packages and relevant characters.
   Third-party components retain their own licences. Compiled JavaScript is
   inspectable and does not guarantee concealment of algorithms.
5. **Services.** Local animation does not include hosting, AI, voice or telephony
   services or charges. Hosting and data-processing obligations require separate
   service and data-processing schedules.
6. **Operations.** No watermark, mandatory account, usage metering or licensing
   server dependency is introduced. Installed applications are not remotely
   disabled. Specify any agreed compliance review procedure, notice and
   confidentiality protections in the schedule.
7. **Existing rights.** Earlier agreements, source access and update rights remain
   in force on their original terms unless the parties expressly identify and
   agree a variation. This agreement does not retrospectively rewrite them.
8. **Term and termination.** Specify term and renewal. Either party may terminate
   for material breach unremedied within 30 days of written notice. Agree the
   consequences for ongoing production, development, distribution and source
   access. Ordinary customers retain use of lawfully distributed products.
   Specify surviving payment and confidentiality obligations.
9. **Risk allocation.** Legal review must complete warranties, acceptance
   remedies, liability caps, excluded losses, IP claims and indemnities in
   proportion to deployment and fees. Nothing excludes fraud, death or personal
   injury caused by negligence, or non-excludable statutory liability.
10. **Law.** England and Wales law and courts apply, subject to mandatory law.
    Signed terms and completed schedules form the commercial agreement.
    Variations require both parties' written agreement.
